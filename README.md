# Pregnancy Runner 🏃‍♀️🤰

A unique endless runner game that simulates the journey of pregnancy while collecting nutrients and managing physical changes throughout the trimesters.

## 🎮 Game Overview

Pregnancy Runner is an educational and entertaining game that follows a pregnant character through their journey. As you progress, you'll experience realistic changes in movement, speed, and physical appearance that mirror real pregnancy progression.

## ✨ Features

- **Progressive Pregnancy Simulation**: Watch as your character's belly grows and movement changes throughout the pregnancy
- **Trimester System**: Experience different stages of pregnancy with unique challenges
- **Nutrient Collection**: Gather nutrients to maintain health
- **Dynamic Movement**: Jump and duck to avoid obstacles
- **Realistic Physics**: Experience how movement becomes more challenging as pregnancy progresses
- **Educational Elements**: Learn about pregnancy progression through gameplay

## 🎯 How to Play

1. **Controls**:
   - `SPACE`: Jump
   - `DOWN ARROW`: Duck
   - `P`: Pause game
   - `SPACE` (when game over): Restart

2. **Gameplay**:
   - Run and collect nutrients to maintain health
   - Your character's abilities will change as pregnancy progresses
   - Score points to advance through weeks of pregnancy
   - Experience different movement mechanics in each trimester

## 📊 Game Mechanics

- **Score System**: Every point equals progress through pregnancy
- **Weeks Progression**: 200 points = 1 week of pregnancy
- **Trimester Changes**:
  - First Trimester (Weeks 1-13): Normal movement
  - Second Trimester (Weeks 14-26): Slightly reduced speed and jump height
  - Third Trimester (Weeks 27-40): Significantly reduced movement capabilities
- **Nutrient System**: Collect nutrients to maintain health and progress
